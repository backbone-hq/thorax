The controller is reconciling namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

The vault-editor, secret-editor, approver, and publisher Roles are intentionally not bound. Bind
{{ include "thorax-kubernetes-controller.fullname" . }}-vault-editor to vault-binding authors,
{{ include "thorax-kubernetes-controller.fullname" . }}-secret-editor to projection authors, and
{{ include "thorax-kubernetes-controller.fullname" . }}-approver to Thorax administrators.
Bind {{ include "thorax-kubernetes-controller.fullname" . }}-publisher only to identities that may
write the namespace's ConfigMaps and trigger vault reconciliation.

Thorax identities and projected plaintext live in Kubernetes Secrets. Before using this
controller, enable Kubernetes Secret encryption at rest (preferably with a KMS provider)
and restrict etcd backup access.

Create a ThoraxVault and inspect its generated request:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get thoraxvault,thoraxjoinrequest
